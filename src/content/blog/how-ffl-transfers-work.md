If you've never bought a firearm online, the process can seem confusing. Can a rifle be shipped to your house? (No.) Is it complicated? (Not really.)

Here's how buying a rifle online works, step by step, so you know exactly what to expect.

> **Note:** This is a general overview of the typical process, not legal advice. Firearm laws vary by state and locality, so check the rules where you live and talk with your local dealer.

## The Short Version

Under federal law, a firearm bought online generally **can't be shipped directly to you**. It ships to a **Federal Firearms Licensee (FFL)**, a licensed dealer near you. You visit the dealer, complete the required paperwork and background check, and take your rifle home.

That's the whole process: **buy online, ship to your FFL, pick up locally.**

## Step by Step

### Step 1: Choose a local FFL dealer

Find a licensed dealer near you that accepts transfers. Most gun shops do, and many pawn shops and some home-based FFLs do too. Call ahead and ask:

- Do you accept transfers from online sellers?
- What is your **transfer fee**? (These vary by dealer.)
- Do you need anything from me before the rifle arrives?

### Step 2: Place your order

When you buy, you'll provide your chosen dealer's information. The seller needs a **copy of the dealer's FFL** before shipping. Often the dealer emails it directly to the seller, or the seller looks it up. At Cybex, we'll coordinate with your dealer to make this easy.

### Step 3: The rifle ships to your dealer

Once the seller has verified your dealer's license, the rifle ships to them, insured and tracked. The dealer logs it into their records when it arrives.

### Step 4: Your dealer contacts you

Your dealer will let you know the rifle has arrived. Some states have waiting periods or extra requirements, so ask your dealer what applies.

### Step 5: Paperwork and background check

At the dealer you'll:

- Show a valid **government-issued photo ID**
- Complete **ATF Form 4473**, the federal firearms transaction record
- Wait for the **background check** (through the FBI's NICS system or your state's own system). It's often approved within minutes.
- Pay the dealer's transfer fee

### Step 6: Take your rifle home

Once approved, the rifle is yours. Inspect it at the counter before you leave.

## Common Questions

### How long does the whole process take?

Once your order ships, delivery to your dealer usually takes a few business days. The pickup visit is typically quick, unless your state has a waiting period or the background check is delayed.

### How old do I have to be?

Federal law sets minimum ages for purchasing from a dealer, and some states set higher limits. **Cybex Precision Gunworx requires buyers to be 21 or older.** See our [Terms](/policies/terms/) for details.

### Can rifles ship to every state?

No. Some states and localities restrict certain firearms or add requirements. Check our [Restricted States](/policies/restricted-states/) page, and confirm with your local dealer before ordering.

### What does the transfer cost?

Each dealer sets their own fee. If you're near Toledo, Ohio, Cybex Precision Gunworx also offers [FFL transfers for $25](/ffl-transfer/).

### Do accessories, optics, and cases need an FFL?

Generally no. Non-firearm items like scopes, cases, and accessories can usually ship directly to you. Only the firearm itself goes through the FFL.
