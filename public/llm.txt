# Cybex Precision Gunworx

> Custom-built precision AR-15 rifles, uppers, lowers, and firearms accessories.

## Company Overview

Cybex Precision Gunworx is a custom firearms builder specializing in precision-machined AR-15 platform rifles, upper receivers, lower receivers, and accessories. Every build is hand-assembled using premium components including 4150 CMV barrels, 7075-T6 billet receivers, and Cerakote finishes. All complete firearms are test-fired and accuracy-verified before shipping.
